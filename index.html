Computer Maintenance Tips

Practical best practices to keep your desktop and laptop running smoothly.

01

Keep Vents Clear

Ensure unobstructed airflow around laptop and desktop cooling vents to prevent heat buildup.

02

Keep Dust Away

Periodically clean dust from internal computer components to maintain optimal thermal dissipation.

03

Maintain Airflow

Position desktop towers in well-ventilated spaces away from enclosed desks or carpeted floors.

04

Keep Files Backed Up

Regularly back up important files and documents to external storage drives or cloud backup.

05

Keep OS Updated

Install operating system updates and security patches regularly for system stability.

06

Monitor Unusual Noise

Pay attention to abnormal fan noises or clicking sounds which may indicate hardware wear.

07

Avoid Untrusted Software

Only download applications and utilities from verified sources to prevent system corruption.

08

Seek Professional Help

Consult professional technicians when hardware problems persist or internal repairs are required.
